# Merry Christmas from the HERizon team

An interactive Christmas card from the HERizon team at Digital Product School, Munich.

A sledge slides in and delivers a card full of gifts; open the glowing one to find two snow globes, one with Munich's Neues Rathaus and one with the team. Available in English, German and Spanish.

**Live:** https://rashida-dudhiawala.github.io/herizon-christmas-card/
